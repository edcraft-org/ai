\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

This project implemented a Python tracing-template authoring workflow with execution-backed checks, revision and reproducible local expansion. In the completed OpenAI comparison, feedback revision produced thirty final technical passes out of forty-five, compared with fourteen for one-shot and twenty-two for regeneration. Its advantage over regeneration remains uncertain. Separate evaluations describe the agreement and response coverage of difficulty and relevance checks, which support review rather than replace educational validation. The next work is website integration, followed by additional domains and evaluation of the complete authoring experience.
