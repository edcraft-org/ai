\section{Supporting comparison tables}\label{app:comparisons}

Tables~\ref{tab:panel-models} and~\ref{tab:repair-models} expand the latest authoring results. Later tables retain historical studies with their original requests and settings. Direct-question generation in the reuse study produces individual questions; one-shot authoring in the main comparison produces a reusable template.

The completed fixtures comprised twenty-four wrong-answer cases, twenty-five colliding-distractor cases, twenty-two cases containing a correct distractor and twenty-six missing-feature cases. Revision succeeded in 24, 24, 21 and 26 respectively; regeneration succeeded in 20, 17, 18 and 21. Cases not reached before the time limit remain unrun. This is a controlled fault study with three correction opportunities after the injected candidate, not another forty-five-job authoring panel.

This earlier study used native tool calling and a different request set. Its later final-candidate reanalysis included five technical passes withheld after judge errors: three timeouts and two invalid responses. The Qwen3.6 authoring trial is omitted from this comparison after frequent response and service failures; its exclusion does not apply to the separate relevance experiments.
